# CityPark Central - Booking Process

## How to Reserve a Parking Space
A reservation can be made through this chatbot. The assistant will ask you
for the following details, one at a time:
1. First name
2. Last name
3. Vehicle registration number
4. Vehicle type (motorcycle or scooter, three-wheeler, car or SUV, small van or minibus)
5. Reservation period (start date and time, end date and time)

## Group Bookings
You can book spaces for several vehicles together, for example for a family
gathering or an event. One person makes the group booking: one first name,
one last name and one reservation period apply to every vehicle. For each
vehicle you only give its registration number and vehicle type.
A group booking can include up to 10 vehicles. It is accepted only if enough
spaces are free for all of its vehicles during the whole period.

## Booking ID and Pass IDs
Every booking gets a unique booking ID, for example PK-7K3Q9X.
Each vehicle in the booking gets its own pass ID made from the booking ID and a
vehicle number, for example PK-7K3Q9X-01, PK-7K3Q9X-02 and so on.
The person who made the booking can give one pass ID to each driver, who shows
it on arrival. For privacy, the assistant cannot look up bookings by ID; for
questions about an existing booking, contact customer support.

## How Far in Advance You Can Book
A reservation can start at most 3 months (90 days) from today, and can last
at most 30 days. Reservations cannot start in the past.

## Confirmation
After you provide all details, your request is sent to a parking administrator
for review. A group booking is reviewed as one request: all of its vehicles are
approved or refused together. A reservation is only confirmed once the
administrator approves it.
You will be told whether it was approved or rejected.

## Cancellation Policy
Reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 2 hours before the start time.
