# Facilities and Services

## Electric Vehicle Charging
CityPark Central has electric vehicle (EV) charging stations on the first level.
Charging stations are available for guests with an EV.

## Fuel
There is no petrol pump, gas station or fuel station at CityPark Central.
There is no diesel or fuel service on site.
Drivers who need fuel should use a nearby external gas station.

## Accessibility
Accessible parking spaces for people with disabilities are located close to the entrance and elevators.
Elevators are available between all levels.

## Safety and Security
The facility is monitored by CCTV cameras 24 hours a day.
Security staff patrol the facility regularly.
Lighting is installed throughout all levels.

## Other Amenities
Restrooms are available near the main entrance.
Covered parking is available on all levels.
Car washing, vehicle repairs and a car wash service are not offered.
There is no shop or restaurant inside the facility.
