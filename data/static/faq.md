# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I reserve a parking space?
You can reserve a space through this chatbot.
You will be asked for your first name, surname, vehicle registration number, vehicle type and reservation period.
The administrator then reviews and confirms your reservation.

## Can I book several spaces together, for example for a family gathering?
Yes. One person can make a group booking for up to 10 vehicles with the same
reservation period. The booking gets one booking ID, and each vehicle gets its
own pass ID to show on arrival.

## Can I cancel or change my reservation?
Yes. Please contact support before your reservation starts to cancel or change it.

## What happens if I lose my parking ticket?
Please go to the exit booth and show your vehicle registration document.
A lost-ticket fee applies.

## Is my car safe in the parking facility?
Yes. The facility has CCTV, security patrols and lighting.
However, we are not responsible for valuables left inside vehicles.

## Can I park overnight?
Overnight parking is only allowed with a confirmed reservation.

## Do you have electric vehicle charging?
Yes, EV charging stations are available on the first level.

## Is there a petrol pump or gas station?
No. There is no fuel station on site.

## Can I wash my car here?
No. Car washing is not allowed and no wash service is offered.

## Are there parking spaces for people with disabilities?
Yes. Accessible spaces are located near the entrance and elevators.

## What if my vehicle is damaged?
Report the damage to the staff before leaving the facility.
You can also contact support using the details in the contact information.
