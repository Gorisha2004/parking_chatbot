# CityPark Central - General Information

CityPark Central is a covered parking facility in the city center,
next to Central Railway Station. It has 4 levels and is monitored
by security cameras 24 hours a day.

## Facility Details
- Entrance: Main Street, Gate A
- Exit: Station Road, Gate B
- Electric vehicle charging is available on Level 1
- Accessible parking spots are located near the elevators on every level
- Maximum vehicle height: 2.1 meters
