# Parking Rules

## General Conduct
Drivers must follow the speed limit of 10 km/h inside CityPark Central.
Vehicles must be parked inside the marked bay lines.
Blocking driveways, exits, fire lanes or other vehicles is not allowed.
Drivers must follow the directions of parking staff and posted signs.

## Overnight Parking
Overnight parking is only allowed with a confirmed reservation.
Vehicles left overnight without a reservation may be reported to the administration.

## Prohibited Activities
Vehicle repairs, car washing and the storage of hazardous materials are not allowed.
Smoking, open flames and camping inside vehicles are not allowed.
Selling goods or advertising inside the facility is not permitted.

## Lost Ticket
If you lose your parking ticket, please contact the staff at the exit booth.
You will need to show your vehicle registration document.
A fixed lost-ticket fee applies.

## Liability
CityPark Central is not responsible for valuables left inside vehicles.
Please lock your vehicle and take your personal belongings with you.
Report any damage or incident to the staff before leaving the facility.
