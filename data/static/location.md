# CityPark Central - Location and Directions

## Address
CityPark Central, 12 Main Street, City Center.
The facility is next to Central Railway Station, about a 2-minute walk
from the main station entrance.

## How to Get Here
- By car: Enter from Main Street through Gate A. Leave through Gate B on Station Road.
- By train: Exit Central Railway Station and walk toward Main Street. The parking entrance is on your left.
- By bus: Lines 5, 12 and 40 stop directly in front of Gate A.

## Nearby Landmarks
- Central Railway Station (2 minutes on foot)
- City Museum (5 minutes on foot)
- Central Shopping Mall (7 minutes on foot)
