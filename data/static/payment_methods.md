# Payment Methods

## Accepted Payment Methods
CityPark Central accepts credit cards, debit cards, cash and mobile payments.
Contactless payments such as Apple Pay and Google Pay are supported.

## Where to Pay
You can pay at the automatic payment machines located near the entrance and exit.
You can also pay at the exit booth with a staff member.
Mobile payment is available through the parking mobile app.

## Reservations and Payment
Reservations can be paid online or at the facility upon arrival.
A reservation is only confirmed after approval by the administrator.

## Receipts
A receipt is available at the payment machine after each payment.
Digital receipts can be sent to your email address on request.

## Refunds
Refunds for cancelled reservations are available if you cancel in advance.
Refund requests are handled by the administration team.
Please contact the office for refund questions.
