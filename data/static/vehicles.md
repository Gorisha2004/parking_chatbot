# CityPark Central - Vehicles Allowed

## Which Vehicles Can Park Here
The following vehicles can park at CityPark Central and can be reserved through this chatbot:
- Cars and SUVs, including electric cars
- Motorcycles, scooters and other two-wheelers
- Three-wheelers, such as auto-rickshaws
- Small vans and minibuses, as long as they are no taller than 2.1 meters

## Height Limit
The maximum vehicle height is 2.1 meters on all levels.
Vehicles taller than 2.1 meters cannot enter the facility.
Most trucks, lorries and buses are taller than this, so they cannot park here.

## Gas-Powered Vehicles
Vehicles running on LPG or CNG gas are allowed, the same as petrol, diesel and electric vehicles.
There is no fuel or gas refilling station on site.

## Electric Vehicles
Electric cars, scooters and other electric vehicles are welcome.
EV charging stations are available on Level 1.

## Prices by Vehicle Type
Prices depend on the type of vehicle: vehicles that take up more space pay more.
Motorcycles and scooters pay the least, followed by three-wheelers, then cars and SUVs,
and small vans and minibuses pay the most.
Ask the assistant for the current hourly, daily, weekly or monthly price for your vehicle.

## Reserving for Any Vehicle
Reservations work the same for every vehicle type.
You will be asked for your name, surname, vehicle registration number, vehicle type and reservation period.
The vehicle type is needed because the price depends on it.
